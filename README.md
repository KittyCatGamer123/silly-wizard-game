## About
### Spell Tomes: Temple of Chaos
A chaotic action rougelike and dungeon crawler, in which you play as a wizard who has trained for years in arcane magic that descends into the depths of the Temple of Chaos. Defeat enemies and collect Essence from them and use the Cauldron at the end of levels to craft or upgrade your tomes, grow stronger as you descend further!

Created with Godot Engine 4.x.
